Our Story

A neighborhood studio
rooted in craft

Petal & Stem was founded by Maria Delgado in 2009 out of a small corner shop on Elm Street. What started as a weekend farmers market stall grew into one of Tucson's most beloved floral studios — known for wild, textured arrangements that feel gathered from a garden, never manufactured.

Every stem is sourced from local growers when the season allows. Every arrangement is made by hand, same day. We believe flowers should feel alive — because they are.
